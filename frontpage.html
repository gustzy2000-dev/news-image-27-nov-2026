Kuwait · Drone Attack

Drone Strike in Kuwait Kills Two Italians

Two Rheinmetall technicians dead in attack on defence site; Iran denies responsibility as region braces for fallout.

A drone attack in Kuwait has killed two Italian nationals, both technicians working for the defence company Rheinmetall, officials said on Friday. The strike hit a defence-linked site in the Gulf state, in an incident that has raised fears of a wider escalation across the region. Kuwaiti authorities condemned the attack and said an investigation was under way. Iran denied any responsibility for the strike, rejecting suggestions that it was behind the operation. Rome said it was in close contact with Kuwaiti officials and was working to bring the victims home.

Ukraine · War

Trump Presses Putin to Pull Back as Zelenskyy Warns of New Mass Strike

Donald Trump pressed Vladimir Putin to pull Russian forces back in Ukraine on Friday, as Volodymyr Zelenskyy warned that Moscow is preparing a new mass strike on Ukrainian energy facilities. Kyiv said air defences were on high alert and urged residents to prepare for long power cuts.

Gulf · Shipping

Iran Says Tankers Hit Mines in Strait of Hormuz

Iran said oil tankers were struck by naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz, raising the threat to shipping through the world’s most important oil chokepoint. Insurers warned of higher war-risk premiums as tanker traffic slowed and several vessels altered course.

United States

Thanksgiving Storms Leave About One Million Without Power

Thanksgiving storms left about one million homes and businesses without power across the United States, as heavy rain, snow and high winds disrupted holiday travel. Utilities said crews were working through the weekend to restore electricity.

Sahel · Niger

At Least 13 Niger Soldiers Killed Near Mali Border

At least 13 Nigerien soldiers were killed in an attack near the border with Mali, the army said, in the latest deadly assault on security forces in the Sahel. No group has claimed responsibility for the ambush on a patrol in the border region.

Europe · Central Bank

Pablo Hernández de Cos Chosen as Next ECB President

Pablo Hernández de Cos has been chosen as the next president of the European Central Bank, succeeding Christine Lagarde. The former Bank of Spain governor will take charge of eurozone monetary policy at a delicate moment for growth and inflation.
